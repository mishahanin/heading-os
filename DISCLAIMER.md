No control is perfect, so the position is stated in advance rather than improvised

## 5. The published engine holds no real data, by design

Real operator data is never present in the published repository. It lives in a
separate, private store that is never published. This is a structural property of
the system enforced by code on the publishing path, not a convention anyone has to
remember:

- a routing map declares, per path, what may be shared and what may not;
- write-time guards refuse content that would place private material into the
  shareable tree;
- a content scan runs on every push, on the only sanctioned publishing path, with
  no override flag, and refuses the push outright on a match.

It follows that an example resembling something real is a coincidence of naming
rather than a disclosure of records. Should a genuine disclosure ever occur
notwithstanding these controls, section 6 applies to it at the highest priority.
